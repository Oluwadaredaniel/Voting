Process

Voting in 4 steps

01

Sign in with school email

Google OAuth restricted to your faculty's student email domain — confirms you're eligible without a separate registration.

02

Browse nominees per category

Each award category displays all its nominees with photos. Scroll through, take your time.

03

Confirm before it's final

Tapping a nominee opens a confirmation step. Votes are permanent once confirmed — no take-backs.

04

Done — your vote is locked

The database marks you as voted per category. Coming back or opening another tab won't create a second vote.

Transparency

How we keep it fair

Transparent doesn't mean public tallies during voting — it means the process is fully auditable.

✓

Verified identity

Every vote is tied to a real school email account — not a name you type, an account you're actually signed into.

✓

Database-level enforcement

The database refuses a second vote in the same category at the constraint level — not just a UI check.

✓

Full audit log

The organizing committee can audit every vote — who cast it, when, for who — at any time after voting closes.

✓

No insider advantage

The admin cannot vote on behalf of others. Their own school email is subject to the same one-vote rule.
